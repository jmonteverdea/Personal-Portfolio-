About

I'm a Certified Energy Manager with an MSc in Environmental Science and 5+ years delivering clean energy programs in Canada and Mexico. My work spans 200+ distributed solar installations, 500+ residential energy consultations, $200K+ in approved CleanTech funding for 30+ clients, and the publication of Mexico's first national energy efficiency guide for extreme-heat climates.

I work at the intersection of technical depth and program execution — guiding homeowners through retrofit planning and rebate navigation, coordinating multi-party contractor timelines, and translating complex building science into accessible program delivery. CEM-credentialed, fluent in English and Spanish, comfortable in front of utility account teams, municipal councils, federal program officers, and homeowners. International field experience in Canada, Mexico, Australia, and Papua New Guinea.

I'm based in Ottawa and relocating permanently to British Columbia. Currently open to energy efficiency program coordination, residential retrofit delivery, and decarbonization roles at Canadian utilities, government agencies, and mission-driven organizations.
