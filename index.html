About

The thread is the same. Take dense technical material, build the systems and training that let people act on it, then measure what gets delivered.

I help governments and clean energy programs translate technical policy into trained communities, funded retrofits, and projects that actually ship.

Over the past five years I have designed and delivered capacity development work across two countries. In Mexico I authored the country's first national energy efficiency guide for extreme-heat climates and trained 150 municipal inspectors and households on ASHRAE 90.1 equivalent standards. At Bright Inc. I scaled a residential solar program to 200+ installations totaling 3 MW, then moved into enterprise where I led USD 1 to 2 million projects across a 25-partner delivery network.

In Canada I now scope decarbonization and workforce roadmaps for 30 active clients at Granted Consulting and have coordinated CAD 200K+ in approved funding. I also led a low-carbon technology assessment for the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority, shortlisting three priority pathways from seven candidates.

Based in Ottawa. Open to advisory work, fractional roles, and program design for municipalities, utilities, and conservation authorities.

Account Manager, Cleantech & Natural Resources, Green Program Delivery

Cleantech and natural resource clients across Canada face a fragmented landscape of decarbonization and workforce funding programs, each with its own eligibility, technical documentation, and reporting requirements. 04 / 05

CEDES. Comisión de Ecología y Desarrollo Sustentable

Project Director, Energy Efficiency

July 2019 to June 2020. Mexico.

150+ inspectors and households trained. Mexico's first national guide.

Challenge

Mexico had no national residential energy efficiency standard adapted to extreme-heat regions. Municipal inspectors and households in hot climates were operating without practical, climate-specific guidance. Existing references were not localized, not in Spanish at the level required, and not connected to a training pathway.

Approach

Government-funded research program. Authored the country's first national energy efficiency guide for extreme-heat climates. Built cost-benefit analyses for insulation and HVAC bundles. Designed and delivered a training curriculum aligned to ASHRAE 90.1 equivalent standards.

Outcome

150+trained on ASHRAE 90.1 eq.
1stnational guide of its kind
2020published

05 / 05

Toronto and Region Conservation Authority

Project Lead, Newcomer Youth Green Economy Project

October 2024 to March 2025. Toronto, ON. Remote.

7 3 low-carbon pathways evaluated, three shortlisted

Challenge

TRCA needed an evidence-based shortlist of low-carbon technology pathways for newcomer-youth green economy programming, aligned to Canada's 2030 emissions targets. The constraints: a 9-person research team, a fixed timeline, and outputs that internal decision-makers could act on without rework.

Approach

Built a structured work plan with explicit quality assurance gates. Evaluated seven low-carbon technology pathways against cost, risk, and feasibility criteria. Synthesized findings into technical summaries and briefing materials that mapped directly to internal decision points.

Outcome

9person team co-led
7pathways evaluated
3shortlisted for action
On timeQA-verified consistency
