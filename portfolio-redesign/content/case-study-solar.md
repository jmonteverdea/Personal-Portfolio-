# Case Study: Solar Program Delivery

**Role:** Renewable Energy Advisor and Regional Manager, Bright Inc.
**Dates:** July 2020 to August 2024
**Location:** Mexico

> **Note to Jorge:** This is the throughput-and-volume cut of your solar work, distinct from the leadership / scaling story in `case-study-bright.md`. If you want to merge the two, say so and I'll consolidate. Otherwise this one focuses on the headline number: 200+ projects, 3 MW.

## Challenge
Distributed PV at volume in Mexico means navigating utility interconnection, permitting, and environmental compliance for every site, often under inconsistent local procedures. Quality has to hold across hundreds of installations or the program loses trust with both clients and utilities.

## Approach
Standardized documentation and approval workflows so every project moved through the same checkpoints. Built durable relationships with utility counterparts to keep interconnection timelines predictable. Managed environmental compliance at the site level. Ran 500+ technical consultations to convert design intent into deliverable systems.

## My role
Owned project delivery from feasibility through installation. Permitting, utility coordination, environmental compliance, and client-side technical consultation.

## Outcome
- **200+ distributed PV projects** delivered
- **3 MW total capacity** deployed
- **500+ technical consultations** with residential and commercial clients on efficiency, solar, and system design
- **~90% on-time completion** on the 150-installation residential cohort
