# Case Study: Granted Consulting

**Role:** Account Manager, Cleantech & Natural Resources, Green Program Delivery
**Dates:** June 2025 to Present
**Location:** Vancouver, BC (Remote)

## Challenge
Cleantech and natural resource clients across Canada face a fragmented landscape of decarbonization and workforce funding programs, each with its own eligibility, technical documentation, and reporting requirements. Most clients don't have the in-house capacity to translate energy audits and emissions data into compliant, fundable submissions, or to coordinate the multi-party timelines that funders require.

## Approach
Standardized intake. Scoped program roadmaps for each client. Built repeatable workflows for eligibility assessment, technical documentation, and stakeholder coordination. Treated each submission as a project with audit-ready compliance from day one.

## My role
End-to-end ownership of program delivery from client intake through funding approval and ongoing administration. Translated technical inputs (audits, emissions data) into the funder's compliance language. Coordinated internal teams and external stakeholders across multiple Canadian jurisdictions.

## Outcome
- **CAD 200K+ in approved funding** secured across the active portfolio
- **30 active client roadmaps** scoped across decarbonization and workforce training
- Ongoing **audit readiness and regulatory compliance** maintained across multiple jurisdictions
