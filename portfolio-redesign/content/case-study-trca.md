# Case Study: Toronto and Region Conservation Authority

**Role:** Project Lead, Newcomer Youth Green Economy Project (Internship)
**Dates:** October 2024 to March 2025
**Location:** Toronto, ON (Remote)

## Challenge
TRCA needed an evidence-based shortlist of low-carbon technology pathways for newcomer-youth green economy programming, aligned to Canada's 2030 emissions targets. The constraints: a 9-person research team, a fixed timeline, and the need for outputs that internal decision-makers could act on without rework.

## Approach
Built a structured work plan with explicit quality assurance gates. Evaluated seven low-carbon technology pathways against cost, risk, and feasibility criteria. Synthesized findings into technical summaries and briefing materials that mapped directly to internal decision points.

## My role
Co-led the 9-person team. Owned the research framework, the QA process, and the briefing outputs delivered to internal stakeholders.

## Outcome
- **7 low-carbon technology pathways evaluated**
- **3 priority pathways shortlisted** for internal decision-making
- Research outputs delivered **on schedule** with QA-verified consistency
- Technical briefs adopted into TRCA's internal decision process
