# Case Study: Bright Inc.

**Role:** Renewable Energy Advisor → Regional Manager, Residential Solar → Partner Manager, Enterprise Solar
**Dates:** July 2020 to August 2024
**Location:** Mexico

## Challenge
Bright needed to scale residential solar across Mexico while moving upmarket into enterprise. The bottleneck wasn't demand. It was execution. Partner quality varied, on-time completion was inconsistent, and selling into corporate accounts required a delivery system that didn't yet exist.

## Approach
Built KPI-based operating playbooks. Standardized the intake-to-installation process. Scaled and managed a 25-partner delivery network with quality control. Launched a pilot enterprise solar program from scratch and grew it through commissioning of USD 1 to 2 million projects.

## My role
Promoted three times in four years. Started as Renewable Energy Advisor delivering individual projects. Moved into Regional Manager, Residential Solar, where I led delivery of 150+ installations and built the operating system. Finished as Partner Manager, Enterprise Solar, leading the corporate program and partner network.

## Outcome
- **150+ residential solar installations** delivered at **~90% on-time completion**
- **USD 1 to 2 million enterprise projects** delivered end-to-end through commissioning
- **25-partner delivery network** managed across quality, coordination, and execution
- **~40% increase in enterprise adoption** within 8 months of pilot launch
- **6 consultants hired and onboarded** with standardized intake-to-installation processes
