# About / Bio

## Draft (188 words, under the 200 cap)

I help governments and clean energy programs translate technical policy into trained communities, funded retrofits, and projects that actually ship.

Over the past five years I've designed and delivered capacity development work across two countries. In Mexico I authored the country's first national energy efficiency guide for extreme-heat climates and trained 150 municipal inspectors and households on ASHRAE 90.1 equivalent standards. At Bright Inc. I scaled a residential solar program to 200+ installations totaling 3 MW, then moved into enterprise where I led USD 1 to 2 million projects across a 25-partner delivery network.

In Canada I now scope decarbonization and workforce roadmaps for 30 active clients at Granted Consulting and have coordinated CAD 200K+ in approved funding. I also led a low-carbon technology assessment for the Toronto and Region Conservation Authority, shortlisting three priority pathways from seven candidates.

The thread is the same. Take dense technical material, build the systems and training that let people act on it, then measure what gets delivered.

Based in Ottawa. Open to advisory work, fractional roles, and program design for municipalities, utilities, and conservation authorities.
