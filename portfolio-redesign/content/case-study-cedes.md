# Case Study: CEDES (Comisión de Ecología y Desarrollo Sustentable)

**Role:** Project Director, Energy Efficiency
**Dates:** July 2019 to June 2020
**Location:** Mexico

## Challenge
Mexico had no national residential energy efficiency standard adapted to extreme-heat regions. Municipal inspectors and households in hot climates were operating without practical, climate-specific guidance. Existing references were not localized, not in Spanish at the level required, and not connected to a training pathway.

## Approach
Government-funded research program. Authored the country's first national energy efficiency guide for extreme-heat climates. Built cost-benefit analyses for insulation and HVAC bundles. Designed and delivered a training curriculum aligned to ASHRAE 90.1 equivalent standards. Took the work from technical research through stakeholder training.

## My role
Project Director. Owned the program end-to-end. Technical research, authoring, curriculum design, training delivery, and stakeholder engagement.

## Outcome
- **Authored Mexico's first national energy efficiency guide** for extreme-heat climates
- **150+ municipal inspectors and households trained** on ASHRAE 90.1 equivalent standards
- **Cost-benefit models** delivered for insulation and HVAC bundle decisions
- Accelerated adoption of efficiency standards across extreme-heat regions
